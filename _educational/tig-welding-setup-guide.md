## TIG Welder Components

A typical TIG setup includes:

- **Power source** (AC, DC, or AC/DC capable)
- **Torch head** with tungsten electrode
- **Gas cup** (ceramic or gas lens)
- **Foot pedal** (for amperage control)
- **Gas cylinder** (argon, helium, or argon-helium mix)
- **Water cooler** (for high-amperage torches)

## Tungsten Selection

Choose the correct tungsten for your material:

| Tungsten Type | Color | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Pure | Green | AC welding, aluminium |
| 2% Thoriated | Red | DC welding, steel |
| 1% Lanthanated | Blue | General DC work, better arc starts |
| 2% Ceriated | Orange | Low-current DC welding |

## Gas Flow Settings

- **Argon (mild steel/stainless):** 15–25 CFH
- **Argon (aluminium):** 20–30 CFH
- **Helium (thick materials):** 25–50 CFH
- **Argon-helium mix (75/25):** 20–35 CFH

## Arc Starting Techniques

**High-frequency (HF) start:**
1. Position torch 1/8" above the material
2. Depress the foot pedal slightly to initiate the arc
3. Gradually increase amperage as the arc stabilizes

**Lift-start (scratching):**
1. Scratch the tungsten lightly on the material
2. Lift away immediately to establish the arc
3. This method works without HF capability

## Common Issues and Solutions

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Contaminated tungsten | Dipping into puddle | Use proper arc length (1/8") |
| Porosity in weld | Insufficient gas flow or drafts | Increase CFH, eliminate drafts |
| Poor fusion | Low heat input or slow travel speed | Increase amperage or slow down |
| Tungsten spitting | Wet filler rod or high current | Dry the rod, reduce amperage |

## Filler Rod Selection

Match filler rod material to base metal:

- **Steel:** ER70S-2, ER70S-6
- **Stainless:** ER308, ER308L, ER316
- **Aluminium:** ER4043, ER5356

Rod diameter typically 1/16" to 3/32" for most manual welding.
