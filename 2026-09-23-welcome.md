---
title: "Why I Started Reviewing Fabrication Tools"
description: "A quick intro to this site and what to expect."
---

I'm a qualified boilermaker learning to code, and I got tired of seeing tool "reviews" written by people who've never held a welding torch. So I started writing my own — based on what I'd actually trust on a job site.

Expect honest breakdowns of welding gear, fabrication tools, and the odd bit of engineering software, all from someone who actually uses this stuff daily.

New posts go up regularly. If a tool's overpriced junk, I'll say so.
