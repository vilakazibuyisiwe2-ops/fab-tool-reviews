## Key Features That Matter on the Shop Floor

### 1. Premium S2 Steel Tip
Cheaper brass center punches dull out after a few strikes on heavy-gauge steel plates. The NEIKO features a tip made from premium **S2 tool steel**, which is hard enough to withstand repeated impact against structural metal without flattening.

### 2. Adjustable Strike Impact
By rotating the top knurled cap, you can adjust the spring force tension. Loosen it for lighter layouts on thin sheet metal to prevent warping, or tighten it down to get a deep, clear indentation on heavy structural channel iron.

### 3. Non-Slip Knurled Texture
Workshop hands are always covered in cutting oil, grease, or sweat. The heavily knurled chrome-plated shank provides a solid grip, ensuring the punch does not slip off your marking lines when you apply downward pressure.
