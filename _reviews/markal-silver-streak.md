## Why Every Fabricator Should Ditch Soapstone

Standard engineering chalk leaves thick, dusty lines that can easily throw your measurements off by 1mm or 2mm. When you are fabricating high-tolerance structural assemblies, that margin of error is unacceptable. The Silver-Streak feeds a consistent, slim lead that lets you draw sharp, knife-like lines for perfect marking-off accuracy.

### 2. It Never Wipes or Blows Away
There is nothing more annoying than spending an hour marking out a complex pattern on a sheet of steel, only to accidentally wipe half the lines away with your welding gloves or leather jacket. Because the Silver-Streak uses a durable, oil-resistant formula, the lines lock onto the metal. They will not rub off easily, and they won't blow away when you hit the plate with a grinder or compressed air.

### 3. The Lines Literally "Glow" Under the Hood
This is the real magic of this tool. The silver lead features reflective properties that react to intense light. When you strike a welding arc or light up a oxy-acetylene cutting torch, the layout line **illuminates and glows brightly** through your dark welding lens. You can track your cutting path perfectly without straining your eyes.
