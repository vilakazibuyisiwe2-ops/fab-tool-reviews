My name is Buyisiwe, and I'm a qualified boilermaker with a red seal, working in fabrication and welding here in South Africa. My background spans boilermaking, CNC machining, and fitting and turning, and honestly, I fell in love with this field, the fire, the sparks, the craft of shaping metal into something real. This site is where I share honest reviews of the tools and gear that matter in fabrication and welding, from CNC software to PPE. I'm especially passionate about supporting women in this industry, including practical things like proper fitting boots and safety gear that actually work for women on their feet all day, because comfort and safety shouldn't be an afterthought. Whether you're a fellow tradesperson or just starting out, I hope you find something useful here.
