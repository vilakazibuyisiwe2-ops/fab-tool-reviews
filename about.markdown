My name is Buyisiwe, and I'm a qualified boilermaker with a red seal, working in fabrication and welding here in South Africa. My background spans boilermaking, CNC machining, and fitting and turning, with hands-on experience across diverse fabrication environments.

Through Fab & Tool Reviews, I share insights and honest assessments of the equipment, tools, and software that matter in this industry. Whether you're setting up a workshop, upgrading your toolkit, or evaluating new technology, I provide detailed reviews and comparisons to help you make informed decisions.

My goal is to bridge the gap between product marketing and real-world application—cutting through the noise to deliver practical, tradesperson-focused guidance.
